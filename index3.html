How I Work

A streamlined, collaborative process designed for clarity, creativity, and measurable results.

Step 01

Discovery & Research

We begin with a comprehensive exploration of your vision, target audience, and competitive landscape. I gather insights through research, conversations, and analysis to build a strategic foundation.

Step 02

Strategy & Planning

With research in hand, we develop a comprehensive strategy. This includes user journey mapping, information architecture, content structure, and technical requirements aligned with your business objectives.

Step 03

Design & Prototyping

Your brand comes to life through thoughtful design. I create wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, and interactive prototypes that demonstrate the final experience with purposeful refinement.

Step 04

Development & Build

Using modern technologies and best practices, I hand-code your site with performance and accessibility as priorities. Every element is tested across devices and optimized for speed.

Step 05

Launch & Optimization

Your site launches fully optimized for search engines, performance, and user experience. Post-launch, we monitor analytics and make data-driven improvements to maximize results.
