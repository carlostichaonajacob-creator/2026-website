A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before we
say hello.

What does a website cost?+

Every project is different. After learning about the pages, functionality, and content you need, I’ll prepare a proposal with a clear scope and price.

How long does a project take?+

Timing depends on the scope and how ready your content is. We agree on a realistic schedule before starting and keep it updated as the project moves forward.

Can you redesign my current website?+

Yes. Share your current website and what you want to improve. We’ll discuss what’s working, what needs attention, and whether a refresh or rebuild makes sense.

Will my website work on mobile?+

Yes. Responsive design is part of the process. Layout, navigation, and content are adapted and checked across mobile, tablet, and desktop screen sizes.

What happens after launch?+

We’ll discuss handover and any ongoing support before launch. Hosting, domain renewals, maintenance, and future updates are agreed as part of your project scope.
